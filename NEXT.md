- Each row is one pattern, in publish order. Newest-published moves to the "Recently shipped" list.
- To ship the top item: write the full entry (problem, mechanism, diagram, maturity, steal this),
  drop it in its chapter folder, flip its INDEX status to `published`, and move the row down to
  "Recently shipped" with the date.
- One a week keeps the cadence sustainable and the quality high. The index already carries every
  pattern's essence and maturity, so nothing is hidden while it waits.
